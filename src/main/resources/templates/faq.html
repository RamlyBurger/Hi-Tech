Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for membership, online purchase, app installation, promotions, and receipts.

How do I get a free premium account?

Make a single purchase up to RM5,000 to qualify for the free premium account.

How do I install the app on my phone?

Connect to Wi-Fi, then search for Hi-Tech in the Play Store or App Store.

What if I forgot to photograph my receipt?

Reach out to the nearest Hi-Tech branch and request another receipt copy.

How do I apply for a free gift during a promotion?

Purchase the Elite membership package and follow the promotion notification instructions.

How do I make an online purchase?

Browse products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout with simulated payment.
