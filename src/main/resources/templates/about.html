About Hi-Tech

Hi-Tech Company is an electronics gadget reseller in Malaysia, founded by TAR University College graduates and focused on practical technology for customers of every age.

Our store

Hi-Tech sells laptops, desktops, tablets, smartphones, computer hardware, and accessories from brands such as ASUS, Dell, Lenovo, Apple, Acer, HP, Samsung, OPPO, Kingston, and JBL.

The original assignment positions the business as a local electronics reseller with 50 retail stores, a KL headquarters, delivery support, and after-sales services.
